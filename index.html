EM Services Staff Handover Portal

A full-stack web portal that helps EM Services, a Singapore real-estate group, manage staff handovers. When employees join, leave or go on leave, the portal makes sure their work is passed on in a clear, trackable way.

Features
Role-based dashboards. Incoming staff get a readiness score, a first-week checklist and a downloadable PDF handover pack; outgoing staff get a departure countdown and tools to write their handover; HR approves onboarding and leave-coverage requests. Also a staff directory, ticket tracking and a live HDB resale price snapshot from data.gov.sg.
My role
Developed the Case Records module, which tracks the property cases each officer handles (client, property address, case type, status, priority and current handler), so ongoing work is handed over without anything falling through the cracks. Designed the core cases data model used across the app and documented its use cases, API endpoints and database schema.
Team
Built by a team of five. Secured with JWT authentication and bcrypt password hashing, and backed by 568 automated tests.

Eggotchi

A sustainability project that tackles food and plastic waste in canteens across Singapore's secondary schools, junior colleges, polytechnics and ITEs by making waste reduction something students actually care about.

The pet
A virtual pet that works like a classic Tamagotchi: it needs regular care to stay happy and healthy, grows when it's looked after, and struggles when it's neglected.
The twist
Students care for it through their habits at the canteen booths. Reducing food and plastic waste keeps the pet thriving, while wasteful habits make it suffer, turning an easy-to-ignore issue into a shared, game-like responsibility.
Beyond that
It teaches students how to recycle properly, including sorting waste into the right streams, and a built-in composting system turns food waste into something useful instead of sending it straight to the bin.
My role
Designed the system and built a low-cost physical prototype, working within a tight budget to keep the concept practical enough for real school canteens.
